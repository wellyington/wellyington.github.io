Most grids hide. Mine stays on screen as a set of hairlines, because a
visible structure makes the layout easy to read at a glance.

## twelve columns, one rail

Content sits on a rail that spans ten of the twelve columns. The outer
two act as margins and keep long lines from running wall to wall.

## lowercase, with a period

Headings are written in lowercase and end with a period. It reads like a
statement rather than a sign.
