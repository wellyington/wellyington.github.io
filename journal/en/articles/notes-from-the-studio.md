A track and a homepage have more in common than you'd think. Both need
a hook in the first few seconds, and both get worse when you add one
more layer.

## the mix is the edit

In the studio, most of the work is taking things out. The same goes for
a layout: mute until it breaks, then bring back the last thing you muted.
