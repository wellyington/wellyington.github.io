This journal is where the working notes live: what went into a build,
what I'd do differently, and the small decisions that never make it into
a case study.

## why a journal

Client work ends at launch. The thinking behind it usually doesn't get
written down anywhere. This is the place for that.

## what you'll find

- **Build:** stacks, speed and the boring parts that make sites last.
- **Design:** type, grids and why fewer choices read better.
- **Strategy:** turning a brief into something you can measure.
- **Music:** notes from the studio, now and then.
