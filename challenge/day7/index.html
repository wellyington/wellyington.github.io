A note from the family

Named after the god of dreams.

Morfeas is a family-run hotel at the southern tip of Corfu, a few minutes' walk from Kavos' famous main street and a minute from the Ionian Sea.

By day, it's the pool: loungers, cold drinks and our resident DJ. By night, it's dinner from our own kitchen, then the party — here or on the strip. Stay a week and you'll leave feeling like part of the family.

— The Morfeas family
