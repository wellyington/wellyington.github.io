How it works

Simple, clear and supportive from the very beginning

Here is what happens when you enquire.

  1. 1

    Step 1

    Enquire

    Get in touch via email or social media with a little information about your business and the support you are looking for.

    You do not need to know exactly what support you need at this stage. We can talk that through together.

  2. 2

    Step 2

    Discovery call

    A short, informal conversation to help us decide whether working together is the right fit.

    There is no pressure and no obligation to proceed — just a friendly and honest conversation.

    What we will talk through
    • Your business and current priorities
    • The tasks you would like support with
    • What is taking up most of your time
    • How much support you may need
    • How you currently work and communicate
    • Which service, package or retainer may suit you best
  3. 3

    Step 3

    Proposal & next steps

    After the call, I send across a clear proposal, giving you time to review everything before making a decision.

    Your proposal outlines
    • The support discussed
    • Recommended package or service option
    • Pricing
    • Suggested next steps
    • Any information needed to get started
  4. 4

    Step 4

    Booking form, contract & invoice

    Once you are happy to proceed, I send over the relevant booking form, contract and invoice.

    This ensures everything is clear, professional and agreed before work begins.

  5. 5

    Step 5

    Onboarding

    We set up the tools, access and communication channels needed for me to support you effectively.

    This may include
    • Access to relevant systems or platforms
    • Shared folders or documents
    • Trello board or task tracker
    • Communication preferences
    • Key business information
    • Guidance for responses, processes or tone of voice
  6. 6

    Step 6

    Support begins

    Once onboarding is complete, I begin supporting your business behind the scenes.

    You will receive clear communication, regular updates and organised support designed to make your workload feel lighter and easier to manage.
