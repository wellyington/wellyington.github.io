From complete beginners to experienced movers, our timetable offers something for every stage of your Pilates journey.

Beginners
Reformer

The place to start if you’re new to Reformer Pilates or returning after a break. In small groups, certified instructors help you master the essential principles and correct technique – and let you know when you’re ready for Intermediate.

Intermediate
Reformer

For clients with a solid foundation, ready for more dynamic movements, longer sequences and greater precision. Progression isn’t about moving faster – it’s about moving better, with control.

Advanced
Reformer

For experienced clients with at least a year on the Reformer. Complex patterns and seamless transitions that test strength, stability, endurance and control – every movement performed with intention, not momentum.

Private &
Semi-Private

Our speciality – from sports performance and rehabilitation to posture correction, pregnancy, spinal health and injury recovery. Private sessions are booked via WhatsApp; Semi-Private directly in our app.
