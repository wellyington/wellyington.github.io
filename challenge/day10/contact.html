Frequently asked questions & answers

  • I’m new – where do I start?

    With a Consultation Class. In this one-to-one session we assess your movement, discuss your goals, identify any previous injuries or limitations and recommend the most suitable classes for you.

  • How do I book a private session?

    Message us on WhatsApp at +34 648 876 173. We match you with the instructor best suited to your goals – from sports performance and rehabilitation to pregnancy and spinal health.

  • Is the studio for women only?

    No – at Nordic Balance Studios we welcome both men and women. We also run Teen Girls Pilates and Longevity (50+) sessions.

  • When can I move up a level?

    Your instructor will let you know when you’re ready for Intermediate. Advanced Reformer is for clients with at least a year of Reformer experience.

  • How do I book group and semi-private classes?

    Directly in the Nordic Balance Studios app, where you’ll find the full weekly timetable.
