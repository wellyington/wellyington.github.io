A little about me

about.

I’m Wellyington — Wellington Almeida — a web developer, designer and digital strategist. I was born in Brazil, raised in London, and I live in Estepona, on the Costa del Sol. Home here is me and my son. He is three, and I am raising him on my own.

I studied advertising, film and photography at the School of Visual Arts in New York. Right after graduating I moved fully into digital branding and development, and that has been my work ever since. Across about fifteen years I have built websites and digital products for businesses in Brazil, then in Barcelona and Ibiza, and now along this coast.

I work best close to people. I listen, I stay with a project, and I want to understand the business before the website takes shape. Digital that works, for me, means something clear and useful, made with the people it is for.

Good to know

questions.

Do you work with businesses in Marbella and Gibraltar?

Yes. I live in Estepona and work with people in Marbella, Málaga, Torremolinos, Nerja, Algeciras and Gibraltar, and elsewhere on the Costa del Sol. We can meet, or work together at a distance.

Do you build apps?

Yes. Some ideas need a website. Some need an app people open on their phone. I do both, and I will tell you honestly which one fits.

How do you approach pricing?

I don’t have a menu of packages. We talk about what you need, then I give you a clear price before the work starts. A small site and a custom app are different jobs, so they are priced differently.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Portuguese and Spanish. A lot of businesses on the coast mix them, and that is fine with me. If you want the site in Spanish, including something as straightforward as diseño web en Marbella, we can do that.
