Cookie policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Local storage does a similar job, but the data never leaves your device. This page lists everything wellyington.github.io saves, and how to change your choice.

The short version

The only thing that needs your consent is Google Analytics, which counts visits. It does not run, and sets no cookies, until you press Accept analytics. If you press Reject, or do nothing, nothing is sent to Google. Everything else the site saves is needed for a feature you use, such as remembering dark mode, and stays on your device.

Changing your mind

Open Cookie settings (also at the bottom of every page) to accept or reject at any time. Rejecting after accepting deletes the analytics cookies. You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings, which resets every choice above.

Links to other services

Some links open other services, such as WhatsApp, Google Maps, Instagram or X. They are only contacted if you click, and they set their own cookies under their own policies.
