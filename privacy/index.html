Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data I collect through wellyington.github.io, why, and what rights you have. It covers every page published at that address: the main site, the journal, Brine Club and the 100 Days concept pages. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spain's data protection law (LOPDGDD).

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is Wellington Almeida, who works as Wellyington, based in Estepona (Málaga), Spain. For anything to do with your data, write to welly.almeida@gmail.com.

What I collect, and why

When you send a contact form

The forms on the home page and on Brine Club ask for your name, email, phone number and message, and on Brine Club the plan you are interested in. Before sending, you confirm that you have read this policy.

  • Purpose: to reply to your enquiry, talk about your project and, if you ask for one, prepare a quote.
  • Legal basis: taking steps at your request before a possible contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • How long: for as long as the conversation is open and up to two years after our last contact, so I can follow up on the same project. If you become a client, invoices and the data in them are kept for as long as Spanish tax and commercial law requires (normally six years).

When you email me or message me on WhatsApp

I receive whatever you choose to send: usually your name, contact details and message. I use it only to reply and for the project you write about, on the same legal basis and for the same time as above. WhatsApp is a Meta service, and its use is governed by Meta's own terms and privacy policy.

Visitor statistics, only if you agree

If you press Accept analytics in the cookie banner, the site loads Google Analytics 4. It records the pages you visit, roughly where you are (country and city, worked out from your IP address, which Google Analytics 4 does not store), the type of device and browser, the page that sent you here and whether a contact form was sent. I use this only to see which pages are read and to improve the site.

  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 22.2 of Spain's LSSI). Until you accept, Google Analytics is not loaded at all.
  • Withdrawing: at any time, through Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. Withdrawing deletes the analytics cookies.
  • How long: Google keeps the statistics for the period set in the Analytics account, at most 14 months. Your choice in the banner is remembered for 12 months, then you are asked again.

When any page loads

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. To deliver pages and keep the service secure, GitHub records technical data such as your IP address. I have no access to these records. GitHub processes them under its own privacy statement. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in running a secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Stored on your own device

If you switch between light and dark, or choose a language, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. Your cookie choice is saved the same way. This data stays on your device and is never sent to me or anyone else. The cookie policy lists every item.

Who else handles your data

I do not sell your data or share it for advertising. These providers handle it on my behalf, only for the purposes above:

  • Web3Forms (web3forms.com) delivers contact form messages to my inbox.
  • Google provides my email (Gmail) and, if you accept, Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited).
  • GitHub, Inc. hosts the site.

Some of these providers process data in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, for companies certified under it such as Google and GitHub, or by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time to:

  • see the data I hold about you and get a copy (access and portability);
  • correct it (rectification);
  • delete it (erasure);
  • limit how I use it (restriction), or object to its use;
  • withdraw your consent, without affecting what was done before.

Write to welly.almeida@gmail.com and say which right you want to use. I will reply within one month. If you think your data has been handled wrongly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD), at www.aepd.es, or to the data protection authority in your own country.

Other things you should know

  • No decisions about you are made automatically, and no profiles are built.
  • This site is not aimed at children under 14, and I do not knowingly collect their data.
  • Links to other sites (social networks, maps, the businesses in the 100 Days series) take you to services with their own privacy policies.
  • If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
